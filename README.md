# Starby

A tiny star friend who lives on your desktop. Starby wanders around your screen, chats with you,
drops starbits for you to collect, and loves to play games.

---

## Steps to install Starby (Mac)

### One-time setup: install Node.js (this also installs npm)

1. Go to **https://nodejs.org** and click the big **LTS** download button.
2. Open the downloaded file and click through the installer (**Continue → Agree → Install**). It may ask for your Mac password.

### Download and run Starby

3. Go to **https://github.com/jrbud/starby**
4. On the right side of the page, click **Releases**.
5. Scroll down and download the **Source code (zip)**.
6. When it finishes downloading, double-click it to open it. It will appear in your **Downloads** folder as **starby-1.0** (the number may be different). You can move this folder to your **Desktop** to find it easily.
7. Press **Cmd + Space**, type **Terminal**, then press **Enter**.
8. Type `cd Desktop` and press **Enter**.
9. Type `cd starby-1.0` and press **Enter**. Use the exact folder name from step 6.
10. Type `npm install` and press **Enter**. This takes a minute or two and prints a lot of text. Wait until you can type again.
11. Type `npm start` and press **Enter**. Starby will appear on your screen!
12. Leave the Terminal window open while Starby is running. To close Starby, close Terminal or press **Ctrl + C** in it.

### Running Starby again later

Do steps **7, 8, 9 and 11**. You only need `npm install` (step 10) once.

### If your Mac asks for permission

The first time, your Mac may ask things like *"Terminal would like to access files in your Desktop
folder"* or ask for Screen Recording / Accessibility access. Click **Allow** or **OK**. Starby only
looks at things like which window is open so he can react to it; he never changes anything on your computer.

## How to play with Starby

- **Carry him around:** left-click and drag Starby anywhere on your screen.
- **Throw him:** let go while you're dragging him, and he goes flying.
- **Talk to him:** right-click Starby to open his chat. From there you can play games, change settings and more.
- **Collect starbits:** Starby drops little starbits now and then. Move your mouse over them to collect them.
- **Spend starbits:** open the **Shop** to buy new colours and accessories for Starby.
- **Keep him happy:** Starby loves games. He'll ask you to play now and then. Say yes!
- **Watch out for limited events:** star showers, shooting stars, black holes and friends visiting can pop up at any time.

### Games

**Action:** Tag, Pong, Catch, Hoop Shot, Maze
**Chill:** Hide & Seek, Starby Says, Star Quiz, Memory, Asteroid Shuffle

Pick one from Starby's chat (right-click him), or wait for him to invite you.

### The Starby menu

Starby has a little icon in your **menu bar** (top right of your screen). Click it to open the **Shop**,
**Settings**, start any game, or **Quit** Starby.

---

## Tips

- Starby stays above your other windows but never blocks your clicks, except on Starby himself.
- Too loud or too chatty? Open **Settings** to change sounds, Starby's size and more.
- When you first open Starby, a short **How To Care for Your New Starby** guide appears. Click **Don't show again** to hide it next time.
