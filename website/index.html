Meet Starby

Starby is a cosmic little guy made of pure stardust. He wanders around your screen while you browse and ignore your inbox. Talk to him, play games with him, throw him around, dress him up, or just watch him sleep.

Throw him around

Grab him, fling him, and watch him bounce off the edges of your screen. He mostly enjoys it.

Play games

Tag, pong, hide and seek, catch, Starby Says, constellation quizzes. Sometimes he'll come and ask you to play.

He notices things

He reacts to what you copy, which app you're in, your battery, and the time of day. He only looks, he never touches anything.

Naps

Leave him alone for a while and he'll yawn, wander to a corner, and drift off.

Star showers

Every so often the sky puts on a show: star showers and shooting stars rain down across your desktop.

Moods

Spin him too much and he gets dizzy. Poke him too often and he gets grumpy. He's got feelings!

Collect starbits

Starby drops starbits as he goes. You don't click them: just sweep your cursor over them to scoop them up. Try it here!

Bring Starby home

Takes about five minutes. Starby runs on Windows 10 and 11. Tick the boxes as you go!

  1. 1. Install Node.js

    Get the LTS version from nodejs.org and run the installer with the default options. You only need to do this once.

  2. 2. Download Starby

    Grab the zip and unzip it somewhere you'll remember, like your Documents folder.

    Download .zip

    Using git? git clone https://github.com/jrbud/starby.git

  3. 3. Open a terminal in the folder

    Open the unzipped folder in File Explorer, click the address bar, type cmd and press Enter.

  4. 4. Install and launch

    Run these two commands. The first one takes a minute the first time.

    npm install
npm start

    Starby lands on your desktop. Say hi!

Trouble?

  • npm not found: close and reopen the terminal after installing Node.js.
  • Error about requestSingleInstanceLock: start him with npm start, not electron .
  • Starby isn't showing? Check your main monitor; he lives there for now.

He's a good star

Starby never asks for admin rights and never changes your files or settings. He only reads harmless things like your clipboard, the active window's name, battery and the time, so he can react to them. Your starbits and outfits are saved in a small file in his own folder. Nothing leaves your computer.
