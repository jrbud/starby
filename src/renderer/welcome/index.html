hi! i'm starby!

i'm a little star, and i'd love to live on your desktop :)

a little about me

i like to wander around your screen, chat, and nap on your taskbar when i get sleepy. i notice what you're up to and i'll say hi about it. i also love games! ask me to play anytime, or i might ask you first.

sometimes i drop little starbits. catch them for me! you can spend them on new colors and outfits for me in the shop.

how to play with me

  • pick me up with your left mouse button and carry me around. let go while you're moving and i'll go flying! wheee
  • right click me and we can talk. that's where the games, the shop, my settings, and focus time are
  • wave your cursor over my starbits to catch them
  • i have a little icon by your clock too. if you ever need me to go, you can quit from there (i'll miss you though)

what i can see

i like to know what's going on so i can react, but i only ever look. i never touch anything!

  • when you copy something, i see what kind of thing it is (a link, a picture...), never what it says
  • which app or website you're using
  • if music is playing in spotify (i love to dance!), and if you're in a call (i'll be quiet)
  • your battery, the time of day, and when you step away
  • dark or light mode, your monitors, and how hard your computer is working

all of it stays right here with us. i don't use the internet and i don't need an account. i keep my things (my starbits, settings, and stats) in my own little folder, and i never change your files or other apps. the only exception: if you tick the box below, i'll add myself to your windows startup list so i can say good morning.
