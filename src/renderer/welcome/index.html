hi! i'm starby!

a little star who lives on your desktop :)

interact with me..

  • drag me around; let go mid-move to throw me. wheee!
  • right click me to talk: games, shop, settings, focus time
  • hover over starbits to catch them
  • my icon by your clock can quit me (i'll miss you!)

what i can see

i only look, never touch:

  • what kind of thing you copy (a link, a picture...), never what it says
  • the app or website you're using
  • spotify playing (i'll dance!) and calls (i'll be quiet)
  • battery, time of day, and when you step away
  • dark/light mode, monitors, and how busy your computer is

it all stays here: no internet, no account. i only save my stuff (starbits, settings, stats) in my own folder and never change your files or apps. except: tick the box below and i'll add myself to your windows startup list.
